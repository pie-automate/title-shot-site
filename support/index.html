Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email pokebloopers@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Purchases

The full career

The amateur chapter is free. When the promotion offers your first contract, one purchase unlocks the rest of the career, all three fighter slots and coach mode. You can also unlock it any time from Help and settings (the menu button on the fighters screen).

Restore on a new device

Open Help and settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Restoring brings back the unlock. Careers are stored on the device, so a new device starts with new careers.

Packs

Packs are optional, one-time purchases: Turn back the clock (a fighter’s body three years younger and 15 less wear, usable from age 30), cash packs (500, 2,000 or 6,000 in-game cash) and, in coach mode, the special tryout (three prospects with ceilings of exactly 75, 80 and 85). A pack bought outside a career waits in your packs until you use it. Packs are not restored on a new device.

A purchase did not arrive

Keep the app open with a connection for a minute; delivery is retried automatically and never pays twice. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date and the item name. Never send your Apple Account password.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saves

Each of the three slots saves automatically after every action and keeps a previous backup. If a save ever fails, the game shows Retry; tap it before closing the app. You can export a career backup from the fighters screen to keep a copy outside the app.

Coach mode

After the unlock, choose Open a gym instead on an empty slot. Sign prospects at tryouts, give each fighter a weekly programme (try Balanced if you would rather not micromanage), take fights and build a legacy.
