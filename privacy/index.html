Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026. This policy covers the Title Shot app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version: Your careers are saved only on your device. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics (such as “a fight finished” or “a title won”) to help improve the game, with no name, account, location or advertising ID. You can turn this off at any time, and in the EU, EEA and Switzerland it is off until you say yes. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Who we are

Title Shot is made by Dhaval Ajitbhai Shah (the “developer”, “we”). Contact: pokebloopers@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your fighters and gyms, their names, records, choices, game settings and any waiting packs are stored in the app’s own storage on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks Apple’s signed purchase record on your device to unlock the full career or deliver a pack. No purchase information is sent to us.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To learn which parts of the game work and where players get stuck, the app sends anonymous gameplay events to Mixpanel, our analytics service, which processes them on our behalf. The events are:

  • the app opened, a career or gym started, a fight finished (its result, method, level and round), a first professional win, a contract offered, a title won and a fighter retired;
  • a purchase completed, recorded only as the kind of product (for example “full career”), never its price, receipt or transaction;
  • a random identifier created when the app is first used, the app version, and the device model, operating system, screen size and network type, which Mixpanel adds to every event.

We do not send your fighter or gym names, your saves, your Apple Account or any advertising identifier. We turn off Mixpanel’s IP-based location, and we do not build profiles of you or link this data to your identity. Mixpanel receives your IP address when the app connects, as any internet service does.

Your choice: in the EU, the EEA and Switzerland the app asks first and sends nothing until you agree. Elsewhere analytics is on by default. Everyone can switch it off or on in Help and settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats; switching it off stops sending and deletes events not yet sent. To ask for deletion of data already sent, email us with the approximate date you used the app.

We keep analytics data only as long as it is useful for improving the game, and no longer than two years.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default. If you turn them on, the app keeps a local list of game events (for example, which choice you made and how a fight ended) on your device. Nothing is uploaded. The list leaves your device only if you tap Export and choose where to send it in the system share sheet. You can turn diagnostics off or clear the list at any time.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a fight result or export a career backup, the system share sheet opens and you choose the destination. That destination receives what you share, under its own privacy terms. Sharing is always your action; the app never shares on its own.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact details are requested.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo access.
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing for advertising.

Children

Title Shot does not ask for personal information from anyone, including children. Analytics events are anonymous and are not linked to a person.

Your choices

You control everything the app stores: replace a finished career on the fighters screen, clear diagnostics or turn off analytics in Help and settings, or delete the app. You can ask us to access or delete analytics data, or object to it, by email. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pokebloopers@gmail.com.
